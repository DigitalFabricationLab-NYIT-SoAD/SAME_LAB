### Building Performance Relevant Columns:

BIN: building identification number

BBL: code for property borough, block and lot 

sf: square feet, as in floor area of a building

Gross area: total occupied area of a building (in sf)

Btu: about the amount of energy in a single lit match

Site energy use (kBtu): total amount of energy used 

Site EUI (kBtu/sf): energy use intensity, amount of energy used per sf on site

Source EUI (kBtu/sf): energy use also considering energy it takes to get the energy to the site

GHG: green house gas emissions, usually CO2 in context of building energy use

tCO2e: metric tons of CO2 equivalent emissions, allows for comparison of other GHG sources

occupancy type: category of building use, like apartment or office; different types have different CO2 limits/sf.

Energy Star Rating
